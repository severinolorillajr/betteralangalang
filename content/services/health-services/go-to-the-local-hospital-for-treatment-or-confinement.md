# Go to the Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement

_Not yet published for Alangalang._

This page will explain how to access this service in Alangalang, Leyte — the
requirements, the steps, where to go, and who to contact.

Nothing is published here yet because the starter kit shipped this page with
another city's information, and guessing at Alangalang's equivalent would be worse
than leaving it blank. Every detail added here needs a public source.

**Where to source it:** the Rural Health Unit, DOH Region VIII (Eastern Visayas), and PhilHealth.

Know this service? See the repository README for how to contribute a correction.
